Privacy Policy

Spor Takibi (iOS) · Last updated: October 11, 2026

In short: Spor Takibi does not collect, store or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What data do we collect?

None. The app has no account, sign-up, ads, analytics or tracking. Your program, completed exercises, calorie log, profile details (age, height, weight, etc.) and badges are stored only in the app's folder on your device. They are never sent to us, and we cannot see them.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, ad networks or analytics services. If you use the online food search (Open Food Facts), only the food name you type is sent to Open Food Facts to return results. The request contains no name, account or device identifier. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy applies: openfoodfacts.org/privacy.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

In the version of the app with Health integration, workouts are written to Apple Health, and data such as steps and energy is read, only if you allow it. This data stays on your device between Health and the app. It is never sent to any server, never used for advertising or marketing, and never uploaded to iCloud by the app. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Notifications and alarms

Workout reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No server push notifications are sent. Permission is requested only when you turn a reminder on.

Backups

If you export your data, the file goes where you choose (Files, AirDrop, etc.). Where you share it is up to you.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device deletes all of its data. We hold no data about you to delete.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age.

Health notice

Calorie figures and program suggestions are general estimates, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, consult your doctor before starting a new program.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
