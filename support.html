Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. There is no account or server, so your data does not move to other devices on its own.

How do I move my data to a new phone?

In the app's backup section, use "Dışa aktar" (Export) to create a .json file, then choose that file with "İçe aktar" (Import) on the new device. Your device backup (iCloud or computer) also includes the app's data.

My program is empty. Where do I start?

On the Program tab, open "Sana özel program" (a program made for you) and choose your goal, level and equipment; the app suggests a weekly program. You can also add exercises to each day yourself in the weekly plan view.

I can't train today. What happens?

Use "Bugün yapamayacağım" (I can't today) to move the workout to another day or skip it. Your plan stays intact.

How is the calorie target calculated?

With the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, from your age, height, weight, sex and activity level, then adjusted for your goal (lose, maintain, gain). It is an estimate, not medical advice.

Reminders don't arrive.

Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → Spor Takibi, and that a Focus mode isn't silencing the app.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your device. All of its data is deleted with it.
